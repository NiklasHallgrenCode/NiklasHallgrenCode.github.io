Summary

Key calls

  • Witch Doctor becomes the default safe-lane supportIt is the best lane partner for Luna, Spectre, Lone Druid and Slark, and the most-picked pos 5 in pro games. Spectre + Witch Doctor wins 54% at Divine/Immortal. Lion, Ancient Apparition, Largo and Shadow Shaman all lose Luna’s lane more often than they win it.
  • Night Stalker is the strongest hero you own52.9% at Divine/Immortal (6th of 127 offlaners), 69% in pro games, and both Lifestealer and Necrophos lose to it. If it survives the phase 1 bans, take it, even ahead of Luna.
  • Luna is a pick, not the planYou are 6–1 with her, but she is average this patch (50.2% in pubs, 42% in pro games) and the weakest laner in Jorel’s pool. Opponents now ban her in half your games. Spectre (2–0, 52.8%) has the better numbers.
  • Mid stays the last pick, OD by defaultOD has the best lane of any mid in bUxU’s pool (+40) and Lifestealer wins only 43% against it. Go DK into Invoker, Puck, Pangolier or Void Spirit. Drop Pangolier, Sand King, Queen of Pain and Ember Spirit as mids.
  • Take Bounty Hunter or ban itBest pos 4 at Divine/Immortal (56%), best ally of Luna (59% together) and Night Stalker (61%), and one of Luna’s worst matchups (she wins 45%). League teams banned it 38 times in 80 games and picked it once.
  • Lifestealer and Viper are the standing bansLifestealer is contested in 85% of league games on this patch. Viper is the worst lane opponent for Luna, Night Stalker and offlane DK, and second-worst for Spectre. Necrophos, Razor and Undying are the phase 2 bans that protect your lanes.
  • Give Jorel a fourth carryOpponents spend two or three bans on Lone Druid, Luna and Spectre. Ursa (buffed in 7.41f) or Lifestealer would turn those bans into wasted ones.

First pick · 11 of 14 games, 9–2

  1. Bans 1, 2, 5 phase 1

    Lifestealer and Viper, plus the opponent’s most dangerous comfort hero (Phantom Lancer against Dansbanders). Expect slots 3, 4, 6 and 7 to go on Lone Druid, Night Stalker, Luna or Spectre.

  2. Pick 8

    First one still open of Lone Druid → Night Stalker → Luna → Spectre. If all four are gone, take OD or Witch Doctor.

  3. Bans 10, 11

    Protect the lane you just committed to. With Luna or Spectre: Necrophos, Razor, Undying or Viper. With Night Stalker: their best remaining carry.

  4. Picks 14, 15 back to back

    Finish the safe lane: your carry + Witch Doctor, or Witch Doctor + Night Stalker if the carry came first. Disruptor if Witch Doctor is gone. Take Bounty Hunter here if it is still open.

  5. Pick 18

    The pos 4 that fits the offlane: Windranger, Mirana, Hoodwink or Bounty Hunter next to Night Stalker.

  6. Bans 19, 21

    The mids that beat bUxU’s planned hero in lane (see the mid table): Huskar against OD; Huskar, Viper, Necrophos and OD against DK; Kunkka, OD and DK against Invoker.

  7. Pick 23

    Mid. Their last pick comes after yours, so pick a mid that is safe blind: OD first, then DK or Invoker.

Second pick · 3 of 14 games, 2–1

  1. Bans 3, 4, 6, 7 phase 1

    Lifestealer and Viper, plus two heroes from their carry and mid pools.

  2. Pick 9

    Best of Lone Druid → Night Stalker → Luna → Spectre, or a direct answer to their first pick: OD or Night Stalker into Lifestealer, Dawnbreaker or Phoenix into Spectre.

  3. Ban 12

    The strongest lane opponent left for your pick 9.

  4. Pick 13

    Witch Doctor, or your carry if pick 9 was Night Stalker.

  5. Picks 16, 17 back to back

    Complete both side lanes from the tables above.

  6. Bans 20, 22

    Their mid player’s two best heroes.

  7. Pick 24

    The last pick of the draft. bUxU counters their mid: OD into DK, Lina, Queen of Pain, Storm Spirit or Ember Spirit; DK into Invoker, Puck, Pangolier or Void Spirit; Invoker into Storm Spirit or Tinker.

Method

Where the numbers come from

  • League: 138 E-Sparven S6 matches from OpenDota (80 on 7.41f), and your 14 games with positions and lane gold from the DotaAnalyze reports.
  • Team pubs: each player’s OpenDota match history since 13 Jul, cached 10 Nov. Turbo and league games are excluded. Many of these are 5-stack unranked games.
  • Pro: OpenDota explorer, every professional-tier league match since 7.41f (1,244 matches, 1,097 full drafts). Positions are estimated from lane role and net worth, as in analyze.py.
  • Divine and Immortal: STRATZ ranked All Pick, using its own position and lane-outcome ratings. Counter and ally percentages use STRATZ’s current matchup data.
  • Patch: release dates from dota2.com/patches; change summary from esports.gg.
  • Limits: 14 league games are a small sample, so one result can swing a hero’s record. Pub numbers measure heroes in uncoordinated games; Hoodwink, for example, is 47% in pubs but a pro staple. Treat the tables as a starting point for the captain, not a script.
